THE MERAKI STORY

Thoughtful
workmanship.

Meraki traces its story from a small studio in Tai Thong Crescent to its current Bugis Village location, where the team focuses on bespoke hairstyles and genuine customer relationships.

Goldwell Masterstylist

Meraki highlights its Goldwell Masterstylist Award as part of its professional craft credentials.

Vidal Sassoon Training

The team continues professional training in London under the renowned Vidal Sassoon haircutting programme.

Bespoke by design

The salon’s stated approach is to create hairstyles that enhance confidence, reflect individuality and deliver an elevated experience.

Bugis energy

Today Meraki operates from Bugis Village, bringing its hair work into the heart of one of Singapore’s most energetic districts.
