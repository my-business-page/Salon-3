THE HANH APPROACH

Personal hair.
Personal attention.

The strongest publicly supported part of the Hanh Beauty SG story is its home-based, personalised format in Toa Payoh. This page turns that into a useful appointment journey without inventing claims about the owner or salon.

01 · START

Understand the hair.

Chemical services work differently depending on the starting point. Previous colour, bleach, rebonding and perm history can affect the achievable result.

02 · TARGET

Show the reference.

A reference image gives the consultation a concrete target. Pair it with current hair photos so colour, texture and shape can be discussed realistically.

03 · MATCH

Choose the technique.

Rebonding, digital perm, colour, balayage and treatments are not interchangeable. The useful question is which technique fits your hair and routine.

04 · FINISH

Make it wearable.

A result should make sense beyond the appointment. Discuss how you normally style your hair and how much maintenance you want.
